# DUST & weekly payouts

<p class="lede">Everything that grows Corium earns DUST, and holding what you buy earns SPARK. Every Monday the week is paid out in SOL: a community pool by DUST, a hunters’ pool by SPARK, and ten prize draws. Your lifetime DUST is your rank, and it never goes down.</p>

<div class="glance">
<b>At a glance</b>

- **DUST** comes from finishing curves, buying stretches, launching coins that take off, winning requests, funding bounties, trading, your crew, and **for free**: the welcome bonus, quests and today's star.
- **SPARK** comes from SOL you put into Corium and still hold 10 minutes later. More in a final stretch, most for finishing a curve. Once earned, it's never taken back.
- **Payday, every Monday** (weeks run Monday to Monday, UTC): the **community pool** goes to the top 20 accounts with 1,000+ DUST that week, by their DUST; the **hunters’ pool** to everyone with 5+ SPARK, by their SPARK; the **draws** to ten winners by ticket.
- **The pools grow with Corium’s fees:** the community pool is 10% of them and the hunters’ pool 25%, each never less than **1.5 SOL**, and the draws add 30% of the hunters’ pool on top.
- **Lifetime DUST** sets your **rank**, from Drifter to Singularity. Rank opens more on the [Feed](/guide/posting#what-your-rank-opens) and on your [profile](/guide/profiles#looks).
</div>

See it all at **[corium.so/points](https://corium.so/points)** (**Earn**).

## Payday

The **Payday** card on Earn says what this week pays in all and when, and each pool in a sentence:

| Pool | Who shares it | How it's split | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Community** | The top 20 accounts with at least 1,000 DUST that week, 500 of it earned | By DUST, nobody over 30% | 10% of Corium’s fees, at least 1.5 SOL |
| **Hunters** | Everyone with at least 5 SPARK that week (up to 50 accounts) | By SPARK, weighted by your Hunter level, no cap | 25% of Corium’s fees, at least 1.5 SOL |
| **Draws** | Ten accounts drawn by ticket | An equal prize each | 30% of the hunters’ pool, on top of it |

The same account can be paid from all three. Everything lands in one payout, in SOL, to your main wallet, shortly after the week closes. With the floors alone, a week pays 1.5 + 1.5 + 0.45 = **3.45 SOL** (the draws once anyone holds a ticket).

**Corium’s fees** are its share of every Corium coin’s curve fees, the fees its locked pool positions earned, and Corium Trade’s 0.25%, that week. When Corium coins trade more, the pools grow: the card shows the next volume goal and what the pool becomes when it's reached.

## SPARK

SPARK is what the hunters’ pool is split by. It counts SOL you put into Corium and **still hold 10 minutes later**, weighted by what the move does for Corium:

| You | SPARK per SOL |
|---|---|
| Buy a Corium coin, or swap into any token on Corium Trade, and hold it 10 minutes | **100** |
| The same buy in a coin’s **final stretch** (a Corium coin, or a pump.fun coin through Corium) | **200** |
| **Finish a curve**: hold your stretch buys through the coin’s graduation | **300** |
| **Conviction**: buy under a quarter of a Corium curve and still hold it when it graduates | **300** |
| **Launch a coin**: the SOL others buy of it in its first day, counted from 0.5 SOL, up to 5 SOL | **100** |
| **Fund a bounty** that paid other finishers | **100** |

So a 0.05 SOL buy held 10 minutes is 5 SPARK, enough to be in the hunters’ pool, and the same buy in a final stretch is 10.

- **Holding is checked on chain.** When the 10 minutes end, Corium reads your wallet. The SPARK is granted for what is still there above what you held before the buy. Tokens sold or moved to another wallet don't count. A pump.fun stretch buy locked in the program's stretch ledger counts as held.
- **Once granted, it's yours.** Selling later never takes SPARK back. Until the 10 minutes end, a buy shows as pending.
- **No daily cap.** SPARK grows with the SOL you put in and hold.

SPARK started with the week of 2 November 2026. Your on-chain DUST from the weeks before counts 1:1 as lifetime SPARK.

### Hunter level

Your **lifetime SPARK** sets your Hunter level, separate from your rank. A higher level weighs more in the hunters’ pool, up to ×1.25. [Hunter levels →](/earn/play#hunter-levels)

## Getting paid

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>The week closes</b> at Monday 00:00 UTC and its boards are frozen as they stand.</li>
  <li><b>The pools are set</b> from that week’s fees, or their floors if those are more.</li>
  <li><b>Each pool is split:</b> the community pool among the top 20 qualifying accounts by DUST, nobody over 30% (an equal share when three or fewer qualify); the hunters’ pool among everyone with 5+ SPARK by weighted SPARK, with no cap; the draws among ten different accounts, each drawn by its share of the tickets, from a Solana blockhash kept with the week. Every pool is always paid in full.</li>
  <li><b>SOL is sent to each account’s main wallet</b> from Corium’s treasury, shortly after the week closes.</li>
</ol>

### Qualifying for the community pool

You need **at least 1,000 DUST** that week, and **at least 500 of it earned**: finishing, launching, requests, bounties and [free DUST](#free-dust). Trading DUST and the 10% crew share add to your payout once you qualify, but can’t qualify you on their own. That keeps the pool for people who show up and build coins up, not for wash trading.

The hunters’ pool has no such line: 5 SPARK is enough.

## Seasons

A season is four weeks. When one ends, the next starts at once, with nothing for you to do. Only the weekly boards start over; your lifetime DUST, SPARK, rank and Hunter level carry on.

## On Earn

**Earn** shows your star, your rank and how far to the next, your Hunter level, the quests, payday, and where you stand on each board this week. **Leaders** shows the top five of the Community and Hunters boards and you; the full boards, the draws and **How it works** are a tap away. The **Hall of Fame** ranks lifetime DUST, SPARK and P&L.

If you’ve [linked wallets](/guide/account#linked-wallets), their DUST and SPARK count as one account, paid to your main wallet. With Telegram connected, the bot tells you when you enter the top 3 or the paid zone, and sends a recap when the week closes.
