# DUST & weekly payouts

<p class="lede">Everything that grows Corium earns DUST. Every Monday, the top 20 accounts on the board split a pool of SOL. Your lifetime DUST sets your rank, and it never resets.</p>

<div class="glance">
<b>At a glance</b>

- **Earn DUST** for finishing curves, launching coins that graduate, winning requests, funding bounties, trading and referrals, and **for free**: a welcome bonus, a star every day and daily quests.
- **Every week** (Monday to Monday, UTC) the **top 20** with at least **1,000 DUST** split the pool pro rata, in SOL.
- **The pool** is **25% of Corium’s fees** that week, and **never less than 1.5 SOL**.
- **Lifetime DUST** sets your **rank**, from Drifter to Singularity. It carries across every week and season.
- **Season 1 is live.** Its first week runs from Oct 2 to Mon Oct 12, 2026.
</div>

See the board at **[corium.so/points](https://corium.so/points)**.

## Earning DUST

| You | DUST | When it counts |
|---|---|---|
| **Finish a curve** | **1,000 per SOL** | Your net SOL into the final stretch of a coin that graduates that week. Corium coins and pump.fun coins with a bounty. |
| **Launch a coin** | **500**, then **2,500** | When your coin first reaches its final stretch, then when it graduates. |
| **Win a request** | **3,000** | The creator of the winning coin. |
| **Fund a bounty** | **200 per SOL** | When its coin graduates, for the part of your bounty that went to other finishers. |
| **Trade** | **10 per SOL**, up to 300 a day | Any trade on Corium, except a creator trading their own coin. |
| **Refer friends** | **10%** of theirs, up to 5,000 a week | On top of theirs, never out of it. [Referrals →](/earn/referrals) |

### Free DUST

Sign in with X, Reddit or Discord and DUST comes free, no SOL needed. At [corium.so/points](https://corium.so/points):

| You | DUST | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Welcome bonus** | **250** | Once per account. |
| **Connect X, Reddit or Discord** | **200** each | Once per network. |
| **Link the Telegram bot** | **150** | Once. |
| **Join @corium_solana and @corium_alerts on Telegram** | **100** each | Once each. Checked with Telegram, so join with the Telegram account you linked. |
| **Follow @corium and @corium_bounties on X** | **75** each | Once each. X tells Corium about follows as they happen; give it a minute before you check. |
| **Post about Corium on X** | **150** | Once a week. A post from the X account you connected that mentions @corium; put your invite link in it and every sign-up through it pays you too. |
| **Choose a star** | **20 to 500** | Once a UTC day: five stars, a DUST amount under each, pick one. Days in a row multiply it, up to ×2 from day 11. A missed day starts the streak over. |
| **Daily quests** | **30** each | Three a day, the same three for everyone. |
| **Bring a friend** | **150** | When someone who joined through your link connects an eligible account. Up to 3,000 a week. On top of the 10% share. |

Daily stars and daily quests add up to at most **1,500 a week**. Free DUST needs at least one **eligible** account on yours: X at least 30 days old with 10+ followers, Reddit at least 30 days old with 20+ karma, or Discord at least 90 days old. Without one you can sign in and look around, but you won't earn free DUST.

## Getting paid

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>The week closes</b> at Monday 00:00 UTC and its board is frozen as it stands.</li>
  <li><b>The pool is set:</b> 25% of Corium’s fees that week (curve fees plus locked-pool fees), or 1.5 SOL if that’s more.</li>
  <li><b>The top 20 qualifying accounts split it</b> pro rata to their DUST. No account takes more than 30%; what a capped account can’t take goes to the rest.</li>
  <li><b>SOL is sent to each account’s main wallet</b> from Corium’s treasury, shortly after the week closes.</li>
</ol>

### Qualifying

To be paid you need **at least 1,000 DUST** that week, and **at least 500 of it earned**: finishing, launching, requests, bounties and [free DUST](#free-dust). Trading DUST and the 10% referral share add to your payout once you qualify, but can’t qualify you on their own. That keeps the pool for people who show up and build coins up, not for wash trading.

### What 1,000 DUST is worth

It depends on the week. Roughly:

| Week | Corium volume | Pool | ≈ SOL per 1,000 DUST |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 1,000 SOL | 1.5 SOL (the floor) | 0.07 |
| Busy | 4,500 SOL | 3.8 SOL | 0.06 |
| Very busy | 20,000 SOL | 12.9 SOL | 0.15 |

So finishing a curve earns roughly a **6% to 15% rebate** on the SOL you put into its final stretch, on top of its bounties and your tokens.

## Seasons

A season is four weeks. When one ends, the next starts at once, with nothing for you to do. Only the weekly board starts over; your lifetime DUST and rank carry on.

## Your DUST on your profile

The **DUST** page shows your rank, your progress to the next one, this week’s DUST by source, where you stand against the paid line, and what to do next. **Share** makes a card of your rank and week for X or Telegram, at `corium.so/points/<your wallet>`.

If you’ve [linked wallets](/guide/account#linked-wallets), their DUST counts as one account, paid to your main wallet. With Telegram connected, the bot tells you when you enter the top 3 or the paid zone, and sends a recap when the week closes.
