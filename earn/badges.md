# Badges on your name

<p class="lede">A badge is a small chip next to your name, given by Corium for something that helped it grow: being early, owning a Seeker, putting in the work in a week. Some come with DUST.</p>

<div class="glance">
<b>At a glance</b>

- Badges show next to your name on the [DUST board](https://corium.so/points), in the Hall of Fame, on your profile and in the Corium app. Hover or tap one to see what it means.
- Corium gives them. You can't ask for one or buy one.
- A badge with DUST pays it when you next open Corium, signed in: the one-time DUST once, the weekly DUST in every week you open Corium.
- The weekly DUST of **GRINDER** and **WAVE 3** runs until the **last week of 2026**.
- These are not the ten badges you earn in [Today's twenty](/earn/play#badges); those are your own, for things you did on chain.
</div>

## The badges

| Badge | Who has it | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **★ EARLY** | The early adopters: on the board in week 2 | **100 DUST** every week |
| **◆ SEEKER** / **◆ SAGA** | Owners of a Seeker or Saga phone who claimed its Genesis token | **500 DUST** per token once, **100 DUST** every week, and ×1.25 on your on-chain DUST in the [hunters’ pool](/rules/dust#the-pools) |
| **💎 OG** | Here with the founder since his trench days | A mark of thanks, given by hand |
| **⚡ GRINDER** | Week 3: the top 20 of the board, and everyone outside it who put in the most work (days played, SOL spent on Corium, coins launched, posts in their own words, friends who stayed) | **500 DUST** once, then **100 DUST** every week until the last week of 2026 |
| **◎ WAVE 3** | Joined in week 3 and came back to play | **250 DUST** once, then **50 DUST** every week until the last week of 2026 |

New badges come as Corium grows. This page lists them as they do.

## How badge DUST is paid

- **When you open Corium, signed in.** Nothing to claim: the DUST lands the first time you open Corium after getting the badge, and the weekly DUST once in every week you open it.
- **To an eligible account,** like all free DUST: an X account at least 30 days old with 10 or more followers, or a Discord account at least 90 days old, on your Corium account. [Free DUST →](/earn/dust#free-dust)
- **It counts on the community board,** in the week it is paid.
- **The last week of 2026** is the week that starts on Monday 28 December. Open Corium that week and you get its weekly DUST; from Monday 4 January 2027, 00:00 UTC, the weekly DUST of GRINDER and WAVE 3 stops. The badges stay on your name.

## Getting one

Corium gives badges for what moved it forward, usually when a week closes and its board is read: who showed up, who played day after day, who spent SOL on Corium, who launched coins, who brought friends that stayed. Farms and alt accounts don't get them, and a badge comes off an account found farming.
