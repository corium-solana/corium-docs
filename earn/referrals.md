# Referrals

<p class="lede">Bring people to Corium with your own link. Everything they earn in DUST adds 10% to yours, on top, without taking anything from them.</p>

<div class="glance">
<b>At a glance</b>

- Your link: **corium.so/?r=yourname**, or **corium.so/?r=&lt;your wallet&gt;**, which always works.
- You get **10% of their DUST**, up to **5,000 a week** from referrals. They keep 100% of theirs.
- It counts for **new accounts**: anyone who signs in for the first time within 48 hours of opening your link.
- One level: you earn from the people you bring, not from the people they bring.
</div>

## Get your link

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>Sign in</b> on <a href="https://corium.so">corium.so</a>.</li>
  <li><b>Open your profile</b> or the <a href="https://corium.so/points">DUST page</a>. Your invite link is next to your rank; one click copies it.</li>
  <li><b>Pick a code</b> with <b>Customize</b>: 3 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores, like <code>corium.so/?r=moonwalker</code>. Codes are unique, first come, first served.</li>
</ol>

Changing your code later keeps your old ones pointing to you, so links you’ve already shared never break and never pass to someone else.

Any Corium page works with `?r=`: a coin, a request, a bounty. Share the page you’re talking about, with your code on the end. Your DUST card at `corium.so/points/<your wallet>` counts as your link too.

## How it counts

- The link is remembered in the visitor’s browser. When they sign in for the first time, they become your referral, as long as their account is under 48 hours old.
- Each week you get 10% of every referral’s DUST, from every source, rounded and capped at 5,000 a week in total.
- Referral DUST adds to your weekly payout once you qualify, but [can’t qualify you on its own](/earn/dust#qualifying): you need 500 earned DUST of your own that week.
- You can’t refer yourself, including your own [linked wallets](/guide/account#linked-wallets).

## Making it work

- **Post the link with something to do.** A coin in its final stretch, a live bounty or a request with a big prize gives people a reason to sign in now.
- **Use a code people remember.** `?r=kopec` gets typed; a wallet address doesn’t.
- **Bring creators.** A creator whose coin graduates earns 3,000 DUST from that coin alone, and 10% of it is yours.
