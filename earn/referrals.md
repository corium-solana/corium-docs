# Your crew

<p class="lede">Bring people to Corium with your own link. They become your crew: everything they earn in DUST adds 10% to yours, on top, without taking anything from them.</p>

<div class="glance">
<b>At a glance</b>

- Your link: **corium.so/?r=yourname**, or **corium.so/?r=&lt;your wallet&gt;**, which always works.
- **An invite pays in two parts:** **50 DUST** when the friend you bring connects an eligible X or Discord, **100 more** on their first trade on Corium.
- Then **10% of their DUST** every week they trade, up to **5,000 a week** from your whole crew. They keep 100% of theirs.
- It counts for **new accounts**: anyone who signs in for the first time within 48 hours of opening your link.
- One level: you earn from the people you bring, not from the people they bring.
</div>

## Get your link

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>Sign in</b> on <a href="https://corium.so">corium.so</a>.</li>
  <li><b>Open Earn → Your crew</b> (or ⚡ → <b>Invite</b> on a phone). Your invite link is at the top; <b>Copy</b> or <b>Share</b> it.</li>
  <li><b>Pick a code</b> with <b>Customize</b>: 3 to 20 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores, like <code>corium.so/?r=moonwalker</code>. Codes are unique, first come, first served.</li>
</ol>

Changing your code later keeps your old ones pointing to you, so links you’ve already shared never break and never pass to someone else.

Any Corium page works with `?r=`: a coin, a post, a request, a bounty. Share the page you’re talking about, with your code on the end. Your DUST card at `corium.so/points/<your wallet>` counts as your link too.

## Your crew page

**Earn → Your crew** shows how many joined, how many are hunting this week, what your crew has added to your DUST this week against the 5,000 cap, and the join bonuses so far. Under it, everyone you brought, best this week first:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Hunting this week** | They traded or did something on chain this week, so their DUST counts for you. |
| **Active** | They earned DUST this week and it counts for you (they joined before the two-part rule). |
| **Needs a trade to count** | They earned DUST, but haven't traded or done anything on chain this week, so your 10% waits. |
| **Quiet this week** | Nothing yet this week. |

Each row shows their rank, their DUST this week, what it added to yours, and their join bonus.

## How it counts

- The link is remembered in the visitor’s browser. When they sign in for the first time, they become your referral, as long as their account is under 48 hours old.
- **The join bonus, in two parts** (since 21 October 2026): 50 DUST lands when your referral connects an eligible X or Discord account. The other 100 land on their first trade on Corium (any coin, any size), on their next visit after it. Someone who signs in, does the quests and never trades earns you 50 and nothing more. Referrals made before that date were paid whole at the start and keep counting as they did.
- **The 10% share:** each week you get 10% of every referral’s DUST, from every source, rounded and capped at 5,000 a week in total, only in a week they traded or did something on chain.
- The 10% share adds to your weekly payout once you qualify, but [can’t qualify you on its own](/earn/dust#qualifying-for-the-community-pool): you need 500 earned DUST of your own that week. The join bonuses are [free DUST](/earn/dust#free-dust), up to 3,000 a week, and do count toward the 500.
- You can’t refer yourself, including your own [linked wallets](/guide/account#linked-wallets).

## Making it work

- **Post the link with something to do.** A coin in its final stretch, a live bounty or a request with a big prize gives people a reason to sign in now, and a reason to trade, which is what makes the invite pay.
- **Bring them to the Feed.** A trade is one of the [three steps](/guide/posting#the-three-steps) that let your friends post, so the trade that pays you also gives them a voice.
- **Use a code people remember.** `?r=kopec` gets typed; a wallet address doesn’t.
- **Bring creators.** A creator whose coin graduates earns 6,800 DUST from that coin alone (300, 1,000, 500 and 5,000), and 10% of it is yours.

## Partner deals

Callers, trading groups and launchpads that bring traders can get a **partner deal**: a share of what Corium earns from the traders who sign up through their link, paid in SOL every Monday. The offer at [corium.so/partner](https://corium.so/partner) is **25% of Corium’s fees** from each trader they bring, for **3 months** from the day that trader joins.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Counted** | Corium Trade swaps (Corium keeps the whole 0.25%, so a quarter of it is yours) and trades of Corium coins on their curve. |
| **Not counted** | Your own trades, and pump.fun trades (they earn Corium nothing). |
| **Your page** | corium.so/partner shows your link, this week, every week, and every trader you brought. |

Deals are made by Corium: write to [@corium_so](https://x.com/corium_so). A deal pays on top of the crew's DUST, not instead of it.
