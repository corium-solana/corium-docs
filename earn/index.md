## Payday

Every Monday at 00:00 UTC the week closes and its pools are paid in SOL, to your main wallet:


| Pool | Goes to |
|---|---|
| **Community** | The top 20 accounts with 1,000+ DUST that week, by their DUST. |
| **Hunters** | Everyone with 5+ SPARK that week, by their SPARK: buy coins on Corium and hold them 10 minutes. |
| **Draws** | Ten winners drawn by ticket: one ticket a day for each coin you buy and hold. |

## By who you are

### If you trade

The money is in **finishing**: buying the final stretch of a curve, the last 10% before graduation.

- Every Corium coin has a [supernova bounty](/rules/bounty), and some carry [bounties](/earn/bounties) from sponsors as well. Finishers split both.
- Finishing earns **2,000 DUST per SOL** when the coin graduates, the biggest source of [DUST](/earn/dust). Every final-stretch buy through Corium earns 300 per SOL on top, finished or not, up to 600 a day.
- Holding pays [SPARK](/earn/dust#spark): every buy you still hold 10 minutes later, more in a final stretch, most when you finish a curve. SPARK splits the hunters’ pool.
- Plain trading earns 10 DUST per SOL, up to 300 a day, on Corium’s coins and in Corium Trade.

Find coins to finish in the Feed’s **final stretch strip**, on **Coins → Final stretch**, and on **[Bounties](https://corium.so/bounties)**, which lists the coins with SOL on them, closest to graduating first.

### If you post

Posting is free after [three steps](/guide/posting#the-three-steps), and the Start here quests that open it pay DUST. Your DUST is your rank, and your rank opens links, scored calls and pictures on the Feed and lifts your posts on For you. Calls are scored from the price when you posted, on the [callers’ board](/guide/posting#the-callers-board).

### If you create

- Launching is free, and you earn **37% of the curve fees** on your coin after Meteora’s cut, then 75% of its locked pool’s fees after it graduates. [Fees →](/rules/fees)
- [Requests](/earn/requests) are prizes waiting for a coin. Launch an entry; if yours graduates first, you take the creator’s share of the prize.
- Your coin earns you **300 DUST** once others have put 2 SOL into it and **1,000** at 10 SOL; **500** when it reaches its final stretch; **5,000** when it graduates. Winning a request: **5,000**. And SPARK for the SOL others buy of it in its first day.
- Your coin stalling at 80%? Put a [bounty](/earn/bounties) on it. Traders come to finish curves with money on them.

### If you sponsor

- A [bounty](/earn/bounties) pays only if the coin graduates in time. Otherwise every lamport comes back to you.
- A [request](/earn/requests) gets you a bag of the winning coin at its launch price, and **half of Corium’s fees on it**: its curve fees from when you opened the request, and its locked pool’s fees for 30 days after it graduates. No winner, full refund.
- Funding a bounty earns **500 DUST per SOL** that went to other finishers when the coin graduates, and 100 SPARK per SOL of it.

### If you bring people

Your invite link pays **150 DUST** for each friend who joins and trades, and **10% of their DUST** every week they trade, on top of theirs. [Your crew →](/earn/referrals)

## How it adds up

One example: a coin you bought 2 SOL of in its final stretch graduates. It had a 1.2 SOL supernova bounty and a 3 SOL sponsored bounty on it, and you made up 20% of the stretch.

| | You get |
|---|---|
| Supernova bounty | 20% of 1.2 = **0.24 SOL** |
| Sponsor’s bounty | 20% of 3 SOL (after a 5% fee if it’s a pump.fun coin) = **0.57 to 0.6 SOL** |
| DUST | **4,000** for finishing (2,000 per SOL) and **600** for the stretch buys, toward this week’s board and your lifetime rank |
| SPARK | Your stretch buys held 10 minutes, and again for holding through the supernova |
| Your tokens | Still yours, in a coin that just graduated |

The numbers are made up; how each one is split is exact, and on the pages above.

::: warning Nothing here is guaranteed
Bounties pay only if a coin graduates, and you can lose money on the tokens themselves. Payouts depend on the week’s fees and on how everyone else does. Most memecoins go to zero.
:::
