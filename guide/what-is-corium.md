# What is Corium?

<p class="lede">Corium is social trading on Solana. One feed shows every coin, every trader and every move as it happens, and every post you read has a buy button on it. You trade any Solana token from the same place, launch your own coin in seconds, and get paid in SOL every week for what you do here.</p>

## How a Corium coin works

Anyone can launch a coin. It starts on a **bonding curve**: the more people buy, the more SOL sits in the curve, and the bigger its star grows. When the curve holds **85 SOL**, the coin **graduates**: its star goes **supernova**, its liquidity moves into a Meteora pool that is locked forever, and trading carries on there.

The last 10% of the curve is the **final stretch**. The wallets that buy it and hold through graduation split the coin's [supernova bounty](/rules/bounty), half of Corium's fees on that coin, plus any [bounties](/earn/bounties) sponsors put on it. Corium pays the people who finish a coin, not only the people who launch it.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>Launch.</b> No creation fee; you pay only Solana's account rent (about 0.03 SOL). Write a launch post and it goes on the Feed as yours the moment your star lights. <a href="/guide/launching">Launching a coin →</a></li>
  <li><b>Trade on the curve.</b> Every trade pays 1%, split between Meteora, the creator and Corium. <a href="/guide/trading">Trading →</a></li>
  <li><b>Finish it.</b> Buy the final stretch, hold through graduation, and split the bounty. <a href="/rules/bounty">The bounty →</a></li>
  <li><b>Get paid weekly.</b> All of it earns DUST and SPARK. Every Monday the week's pools are paid in SOL. <a href="/earn/dust">DUST & weekly payouts →</a></li>
</ol>

## Built on

| | |
|---|---|
| Bonding curve | [Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve](https://docs.meteora.ag) (DBC) |
| After graduation | Meteora DAMM v2, liquidity permanently locked |
| Bounties, prizes, fee split | The Corium program (`corium_launch`), [verified by OtterSec](https://verify.osec.io/status/NovanpiewpH4zvYgtzAQN2zWQ94KcKWrHCTswWdZ1Y1), upgradeable only by a Squads multisig |
| Corium Trade | Jupiter's best route for any token; the fee is a plain SOL transfer in the same transaction |
| Network | Solana mainnet, at [corium.so](https://corium.so), and in the [apps](/guide/apps) |

Corium never holds your funds. Bounties and prizes sit in program accounts whose rules are public, Cori's wallet keeps its keys on your device, and every trade, launch and claim is a transaction you sign yourself.

::: warning Memecoins are risky
Most memecoins go to zero. Nothing on Corium is financial advice, and a coin, a post or a call appearing on Corium is not an endorsement. Only trade what you can afford to lose. You must be 18 or older.
:::
