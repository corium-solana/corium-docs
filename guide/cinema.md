# Cinema mode

For screenshots, clips and trailers. Click the **eye icon** in the top-left corner (**Hide UI**) to hide everything except the star. Click it again to bring the interface back.

With cinema mode on, you can steer the camera with the keyboard:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `←` `→` | Orbit around the star |
| `↑` `↓` | Dolly in and out |
| `Shift` + `↑` `↓` | Tilt up and down |
| `Shift` + `C` | Play the scripted **pull-back shot**: a 25-second close-up of the star's surface, a tight orbit, then one accelerating spin out to full distance. It holds for the last 5 seconds, leaving room for a logo. Press again to restart; any arrow key cancels. |
| `Shift` + `R` | Replay the last push, with its fall into the star |

Tap a key for a slow crawl, or hold it to speed up. The camera coasts to a stop when you let go.

## Tips for recording

- Set **Settings → Graphics** to **Ultra** for the best-looking star.
- Open a **past star** (`corium.so/star/<number>`) and press play on its lifecycle to capture a full birth-to-nova run without waiting for a live one.
- Use a player with a **callsign** if you want names over the pushes in the shot.
- `Shift` + `C` is designed to be used as-is for an intro or outro.
