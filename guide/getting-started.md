# Getting started

<p class="lede">Open <a href="https://corium.so">corium.so</a> or the <a href="/guide/apps">app</a>. You can read the Feed, every coin, chart and board without an account. To trade, post and earn, sign in.</p>

## 1. Sign in

There are three ways in, and they all lead to the same kind of account:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Connect a wallet** | Make [Cori's wallet](/guide/wallet) in a tap, or connect one you already have: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or any wallet that supports the Wallet Standard. In the Android app, your wallet app connects through Mobile Wallet Adapter. |
| **Sign in with X** | Corium reads your public profile only, and never posts for you. |
| **Sign in with Discord** | The same. |

Signing in with a wallet is a single **message signature**:

- **It is not a transaction.** Nothing moves and there is no fee. If a "sign-in" ever asks you to approve a transfer, it isn't us.
- **It carries your agreement.** The message says you are 18 or older and agree to the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms), pinned to the exact version you saw. Your wallet shows the full text before you sign. Signing in with X or Discord asks you to tick the same words first.
- **It keeps you signed in** for up to 30 days (14 days without a visit). Signing out ends the session at once.

Whichever way you came in, add the others later from your profile's **Account** tab: they become one account. Liking, following, watching a coin and messages work with any sign-in. Anything that signs a transaction (trades, launches, bounties, claims) needs a wallet. [Your account →](/guide/account)

## 2. Pick a wallet

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cori's wallet** | Built into Corium. Made in one tap, keys on your device, locked with Face ID, Touch ID, your fingerprint or Windows Hello. No extension, no app switching: small trades sign as you press Buy. You write down a 12-word recovery phrase once. |
| **Your own wallet** | Any Solana wallet. Every trade opens in your wallet for you to approve. |

Either way, you need some SOL for trades and network fees. Corium never holds your funds. [Wallets →](/guide/wallet)

## 3. Claim your welcome DUST

Open **Earn**. The **Start here** tab lists the one-time quests, each paying DUST once:

| Quest | DUST |
|---|---|
| Claim the welcome bonus | **250** |
| Connect X | **200** |
| Connect Discord | **200** |
| Link the Telegram bot | **150** |
| Make your first trade on Corium Trade | **200** |

Free DUST like this needs one **eligible** account on yours: an X account at least 30 days old with 10 or more followers, or a Discord account at least 90 days old. DUST sets your [rank](/earn/dust#ranks), and the week's DUST is paid out in SOL. [Ways to earn →](/earn/)

## 4. Three steps to post

Reading, liking and reposting are open to anyone signed in. To **post and reply**, show once that you're a person who uses Corium:

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>Sign in with a wallet.</b> Any wallet on your account counts.</li>
  <li><b>Make one trade on Corium.</b> On a coin's curve or in Corium Trade, any size.</li>
  <li><b>Link X or Discord.</b> One of the two.</li>
</ol>

The composer shows the three, ticked as you do them, with a button for each one left. That's all it takes: posting is free, and it stays free. Your rank then opens links, scored calls and pictures, lets you post more a day, and lifts your posts on **For you**. [Posting →](/guide/posting)

## 5. Your profile

Your account gets a public profile with a **random star name**, like *Molten Quasar* or *Quiet Gravity*. Keep it, or pick your own (or use your X or Discord name and photo) from **Edit profile**. Your star, colours and page are in **Customize**.

| | |
|---|---|
| Name | 3 to 20 letters, numbers, spaces, dots, dashes or underscores. Unique across Corium, ignoring capitals. Names that look like wallet addresses or official accounts are refused. |
| Photo | PNG, JPEG or WebP, cropped to a square. Checked before it's stored, and stored on IPFS. |
| Bio | Up to 160 characters. |

Your name and photo replace your address everywhere: posts, chat, trades, holders, boards. [Profiles →](/guide/profiles)

## 6. Find something to do

- **[The Feed](/guide/feed)** is the home page: what people say and what happens, ranked for you.
- **Coins** lists Corium's own coins: Trending, Final stretch, About to graduate, New, Supernovas, Collapsed.
- **Trade** lists every Solana token: Pulse, Trending, Stocks, Big coins and your watchlist.
- **Search** (the field in the top bar, <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl/Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, or ⚡ on a phone) finds coins, tokens, people, posts and pages, and reads a pasted address.
