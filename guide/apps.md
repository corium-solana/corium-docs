# The apps

<p class="lede">Corium runs on the web at corium.so and as native apps. One account works everywhere: sign in the same way on any of them and your DUST, rank, posts, follows, watchlist and look come with you.</p>

| | Where |
|---|---|
| **Web** | [corium.so](https://corium.so), in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone. |
| **Android** | [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=so.corium.launch), and the **Solana dApp Store** on a Seeker or a Saga. |
| **iPhone** | The iOS app is in Apple's review. This page will link it once it's on the App Store. |

The apps are native, not the website in a frame: the stars, the charts and the Feed are drawn by the phone itself, so they run smoothly.

## What's in the apps

The apps carry Corium as the site has it:

- **Home** is the Feed: the coin hero, then **For you**, **Following** and **Coins**. [The Feed →](/guide/feed)
- **Trade** has its own tab: Pulse, Trending, Stocks, Big coins, your watchlist and search, a one-tap buy on every row, and every token's page with its chart, screen, audit, your position and a live quote before you sign. [Corium Trade →](/guide/trading#corium-trade-any-solana-token)
- **⚡** in the middle: your positions with Sell, search across everything, and quick actions. [The quick sheet →](/guide/wallet#the-quick-sheet-and-the-you-menu)
- **Earn**: the quests, payday and the boards. [Earn →](/earn/)
- **You**: your profile as a player's page, your wallet, and everything else.

Posting works the same: the [three steps](/guide/posting#the-three-steps) and your rank decide what you can post, wherever you post from.

## Wallets in the apps

- **Cori's wallet** is built in: keys made on your phone, locked with your fingerprint or face, never sent anywhere. Write down its recovery phrase. [Cori's wallet →](/guide/wallet#cori-s-wallet)
- **Your own wallet** on Android connects through Mobile Wallet Adapter: Seed Vault on a Seeker, Phantom, Solflare. Every trade opens in it for you to approve.

## One account

Your Corium account is not tied to one device or one wallet. It's the logins on it: your wallets, your X and your Discord. Sign in on a new device with any one of them and you're in your own account. Link more from **You → Account**. [Your account →](/guide/account)
