# Profiles

<p class="lede">A profile on Corium reads like a player's page: your star filling the stage, your rank, your record as a trader, caller and creator, and a look you build as your rank grows. It is your account's, every linked wallet as one.</p>

Open anyone's name or picture to see theirs. Yours is under **You**, and at `corium.so/@yourcode` (your [invite code](/earn/referrals)). A profile is also at `corium.so/wallet/<address>` for any of its wallets. Everything on a profile is public, like the chain it comes from.

## The page

| | |
|---|---|
| **The stage** | Your personal star, grown from your account's own key, with rings around it and your coins on show riding them. |
| **The crest** | Your picture, with your rank's ring around it filling toward the next rank, and the rank's numeral (I to IX). |
| **The rank line** | Your rank, its numeral, your lifetime DUST and your [badges](/earn/badges). |
| **The readout** | Your numbers along the foot of the stage. Yours: balance, holdings, trading P&L, and **Ready to claim** with **Claim all**. Anyone else's: holdings, P&L, bounties earned, coins launched and calls. |
| **The rank path** | Drifter at the foot to Singularity at the top, lit as far as you've climbed. On your own, what the next rank opens. |

## The record

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Posts** | Posts, Moves, Replies, Media and Likes. **Moves** is everything the account did on Corium, as the Feed draws it: buys, sells, launches, bounties and supernovas. |
| **Holdings** | What the account's wallets hold now, read from the chain: Corium's coins and every other token (stocks, big coins, other launchpads' coins, stablecoins) worth something in SOL, with profit against what was paid. |
| **Bounties** | Bounties in a final stretch (with any finisher tokens locked there), the supernovas finished and what they paid. |
| **Launched** | The coins the account launched, with their curves and the creator fees they've earned. |
| **Calls** | [Calls on the Feed](/guide/posting#calls), each saying how it has done since, under the 30-day record; then the [callouts](/guide/social#callouts) from coin chats. |
| **Activity** | Trades, launches and claims, as a timeline. |
| **Record** | Your pump.fun track record, once imported. [Track record →](/guide/account#track-record-bring-your-pump-fun-stats) |
| **Account** | Yours only: your logins, wallets, Telegram and sign out. [Your account →](/guide/account) |

Beside the posts, a column of blocks the account picks in **Customize**, in its own order: **Latest moves** (live), **Stars lit** (coins launched), **Top holdings**, **Supernovas** (curves finished), **Followed by** and **Call record** (calls, hits, the typical one and the best, over 30 days). Visitors see only the blocks with something in them.

## Following

**Follow** anyone from their profile or from the ⋯ menu on their post. Their posts, reposts and moves fill your **Following** tab, rank higher on **For you**, and their trades and calls ring your bell. Follower and following counts are public.

**Watch** a coin with ☆ beside its name on its page to keep it on your watchlist (on the Feed, in Corium Trade and in terminal mode), and to hear when it enters the final stretch and when it goes supernova. Your watchlist is private and belongs to your account, on every device.

## Looks

Your page is yours to dress, from **Customize** on your own profile. Every change shows on the page at once. Each rank opens more, and every look comes in any of your colours:

| Rank | Opens |
|---|---|
| <span style="color:#7b8397">●</span> **Drifter** | Your own star, grown by your DUST; star dust in your sky; amber and ash accents; three titles; a wide name; 2 blocks in your column; the tab visitors land on |
| <span style="color:#ff7a45">●</span> **Ember** | A gradient name; meteor showers; a squircle frame; a headline across your stage; links on your profile; 3 more accents and titles; mono type; 3 blocks |
| <span style="color:#ffa35c">●</span> **Kindler** | A neon name; choose between 3 forms of your star; a nebula; a hexagon frame; a second colour; glowing rings; 3 coins on show; **pin a post**; serif type; 4 blocks |
| <span style="color:#ffc978">●</span> **Stoker** | An aurora name; move your star left, centre or right; constellations; 6 star forms; an octagon frame; a ring around your picture; 5 blocks |
| <span style="color:#ffe6a0">●</span> **Forgehand** | A plasma name; an aurora in your sky; an asteroid belt; a diamond frame; 10 star forms; show your star at any stage it reached; the ring turns; 6 coins on show; all 6 blocks |
| <span style="color:#fff4dd">●</span> **Starwright** | A holo name; solar wind; a deep sky; **any colour you like**; 16 star forms |
| <span style="color:#d4e8ff">●</span> **Novabound** | Your star as its supernova; a starlight name; 24 star forms |
| <span style="color:#9dc2ff">●</span> **Heliarch** | An event horizon name; 32 star forms |
| <span style="color:#c6a4ff">●</span> **Singularity** | Your star collapses into a black hole, or shows any stage before it; 48 star forms |

Every rank adds titles to wear under your name too, from *Stargazer* to *The Void*. Your look follows you into the Feed: your frame, ring and name style show on every post you write.

Looks are opened by rank, and rank is earned DUST. They can't be bought. Corium checks every look against your rank before it's saved.
