# Telegram alerts

**[@CoriumAlertsBot](https://t.me/CoriumAlertsBot)** tells you when something happens to the coins and people you care about, and puts buy buttons one tap away.

## Connect

1. Open **your profile** on [corium.so](https://corium.so) and sign in.
2. Tap **Connect Telegram**. Telegram opens the bot with a one-time link.
3. Tap **Start**. The bot answers *Linked to* your profile name.

One wallet per chat. `/stop` in the bot, or **Disconnect** on your profile, unlinks it.

## What you hear about

| Alert | When |
|---|---|
| 🟢🔴 Trades | Someone you follow buys or sells 0.5 SOL or more |
| ✨ Launches | Someone you follow launches a coin |
| 📣 Callouts | Someone you follow calls a coin |
| 🔥 Final stretch | A coin you watch enters its final stretch |
| 💥 Supernova | A coin you watch goes supernova |
| 🏆 Bounty | You won part of a supernova bounty (with a Claim button) |
| ✨ DUST | You entered this week’s top 3 or the paid top 20, and a recap when the week closes |

Follow people on their profiles; watch coins with ☆ **Watch** on a coin page, or `/watch` in the bot.

## Commands

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `/buy HDOG 0.5` | A button that opens a 0.5 SOL buy of $HDOG in your wallet (without an amount: 0.1, 0.5 and 1 SOL) |
| `/finish HDOG` | Finish a coin: its curve, its bounties, and buttons that open its [finish page](/guide/finish-links) in your wallet. Takes a ticker or a pump.fun link. |
| `/sell HDOG 50` | A button to sell 50% of your $HDOG (25% and 100% too) |
| `/claims` | Bounties you won and creator fees waiting for you |
| `/watch HDOG`, `/unwatch HDOG` | Watch or stop watching a coin (a ticker or a mint) |
| `/list` | The coins you watch |
| `/wallet phantom`, `solflare`, `browser` | Where the buttons open |
| `/mute`, `/unmute` | Pause alerts |
| `/stop` | Unlink this chat |

::: tip Buttons never trade
A Buy or Sell button opens Corium inside your wallet app with the trade panel filled in. Nothing happens until you tap Buy and approve it in your wallet. The bot never has your keys.
:::
