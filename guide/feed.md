# The Feed

<p class="lede">The Feed is Corium's home: what traders say and what happens on Corium, in one stream. Posts, calls and replies sit next to every launch, big buy, bounty and supernova, and every coin in it has a buy button.</p>

## The tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **For you** | Everything recent, ranked for you: posts, calls, coin chat, and what happens on Corium. Works signed out too. |
| **Following** | The people you follow: their posts, what they reposted, and their moves (trades, launches, bounties), newest first. Needs a sign-in and at least one follow. |
| **Coins** | Corium's own coins: Trending, Final stretch, About to graduate, New, Supernovas, Collapsed. |
| **Trade** | Every other Solana token, from [Corium Trade](/guide/trading#corium-trade-any-solana-token): Pulse, Trending, Stocks, Big coins and your Watchlist. |

Every tab scrolls on for as long as there is anything older. When something new arrives, a pill at the top says so; tap it to see it.

Beside the timeline on a wide screen: your watchlist or what trades now, the coins closest to supernova, the markets, the best callers, the coins talked about today, people to follow, and the coins that just went supernova. Across the top, the **final stretch strip** shows the coins in their last 10% right now that are worth a look: Corium's own, any with a bounty on it, and others only when their trading reads as organic and the [screen](/guide/screen) doesn't rate them red. Closest to supernova first.

## How For you is ranked

For you works like the big social apps' home timelines, with Corium's own signals added. Each item gets a score for you, and the page is the best scores, mixed so no one voice takes over.

| | What moves an item up or down |
|---|---|
| **What readers did** | Replies and opening a post count most, then reposts, quotes, likes and bookmarks, per view. A reply the author answers counts a lot. "Not interested" counts heavily against. A new post starts as typical until enough people have seen it. |
| **Who wrote it** | The author's standing: followers, [rank](/earn/dust#ranks), lifetime [SPARK](/earn/dust#spark), badges, and a linked X account and wallet. A Drifter's posts sort lower; every rank above sorts higher. |
| **You and them** | Posts by people you follow, or reposted by them, count about **double**. Coins you trade or hold count more; coins whose posts you liked count a little more. Your own posts sink (you've seen them). |
| **How fresh** | A post loses half its weight every 6 hours; an event, a chat line or a callout every 2. Nothing older than 3 days is ranked. |
| **Safety** | Anything about a coin the screen rates red sinks far down. |
| **Variety** | The same author or the same coin counts for less each time it has just been shown, events stay under 40% of what you see and coin chat under 25%, and one kind of event in a row costs more each time. |

A small daily shuffle keeps two people's feeds apart and gives something new a look. Signed out, nothing is personal and everything counts the same.

Rank buys reach, never permission: anyone who has done [the three steps](/guide/posting#the-three-steps) can post, and every post can be seen. Rank decides how far it travels.

### Not interested

The **⋯** menu on a card hides what you don't want: this post, everything by this person, everything about this coin. Corium remembers it for your account.

## Event cards

What happens on Corium is posted to the Feed as it happens, each kind as its own card. A person's doing is posted by that person (their wallet's name and picture); a coin's own moment is posted by the coin, with its picture as the avatar. Every card carries the coin, with its numbers, its chart and a buy button.

<figure class="pic narrow"><img src="/shots/card-launch.webp" alt="A launch on the Feed" /><figcaption>A launch: the new star as a poster, with its coin card and a buy.</figcaption></figure>


| Card | Means |
|---|---|
| **New star** | A coin launched on Corium. Its creator can write the [launch post](/guide/launching#your-launch-post) that goes under it; otherwise Corium posts the launch. |
| **Bounty** | Someone put SOL on a coin graduating. The wallets that finish its curve share it. [Bounties →](/earn/bounties) |
| **Bought** / **Big buy** | A buy, on the record. For you shows a lone buy only from **2 SOL** up; smaller ones show as part of a crowd. Buys of a Corium coin are posted from 0.5 SOL, buys in Corium Trade from 0.2 SOL. |
| **Sold** | A sale of 0.5 SOL or more in Corium Trade. Sales show only in Following, from the people you follow. |
| **Buying** (a crowd) | Several wallets bought the same coin within a few hours of each other: their faces, the SOL and the number of buys, as one card. |
| **Final stretch** | A coin in the last 10% of its curve, with the SOL still to go before it goes supernova. |
| **Supernova** | A coin graduated: its curve is complete, and it trades on its pool now. |
| **Bounty paid** | A bounty paid out to the wallets that finished the coin. |

The trades of people you follow show one card each, as they happened. Everyone else's are gathered as above, so the Feed reads as news, not a log.

Coin chat and [callouts](/guide/social#callouts) from coin pages come into For you too, marked as such.

## Buying from a post

Every coin on the Feed has **Ape in**. It opens a small buy sheet over the Feed: the coin's price, its day and its market cap, your preset amounts and a box for any amount.

- A Corium coin still on its curve is bought on its curve. Anything else goes through [Corium Trade](/guide/trading#corium-trade-any-solana-token) on the best route, for 0.25%.
- A coin the [screen](/guide/screen) rates red shows its red flags before you buy. You decide; nothing is blocked.
- With **one-tap trades** on in the trade settings, a preset buys as you tap it. Through [Cori's wallet](/guide/wallet#two-taps), the first tap arms the button and a second within 3 seconds buys.
- Nothing is sent without your wallet. Your own wallet asks you to approve it; Cori's wallet signs within your instant limits.

When the buy lands, Corium offers to **post it**. A post made then is a [call](/guide/posting#calls), scored from the price at that moment.

## Reading a post

| | |
|---|---|
| **$TAG** | A coin. Tap it for its card and a buy. |
| **@name** | A person, by their X or Discord handle or their name. Tap it for their profile. |
| **Dev** | Posted by the coin's creator, about their own coin. |
| **4.2× since posted** | How the call has done since it was posted, with the market cap then and now. A call at 2× or better is marked as a hit, one under half its price as a miss. |
| Reply, repost, like, views | As on X. A quote is a repost with your own words. **Bookmark** keeps it for you alone. |

Opening a post shows it with its replies in threads. Views are counted once per reader per day.

## Notifications

The **bell** collects what's for you: likes, reposts, replies, quotes and mentions of your posts, new followers, the trades and calls of the people you follow, supernovas of coins you watch, and bounties. Get the same on [Telegram](/guide/telegram).
