# Profile, wallets & track record

<p class="lede">Your Corium account is your wallet, your public profile, and everything you’ve done on chain. Link more wallets to make them one account, and bring your pump.fun history with you.</p>

## Your profile

Every wallet that signs in gets a public profile at `corium.so/wallet/<address>`: a star name (random at first, yours to change), a photo, a bio, your [DUST rank](/earn/dust#ranks), and tabs for what you’ve done.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Holdings** | Coins you hold, with value and profit against your average cost |
| **Bounties** | Supernova bounties and sponsored bounties you’ve earned, settling or ready to claim |
| **Created** | Coins you launched, and the creator fees they’ve earned |
| **Calls** | Your [callouts](/guide/social#callouts) and how they did |
| **Activity** | Your trades, launches and claims |
| **Track record** | Your pump.fun history (below), once imported |

Setting up your name and photo: [Getting started →](/guide/getting-started#_3-your-profile)

### Ready to claim

On your own profile, one card adds up your supernova bounties and creator fees, and **Claim all** collects them in as few transactions as possible. Payouts from sponsored bounties and requests (finisher shares, prizes, a request’s stake, refunds) are on the **Bounties** tab and on each bounty’s or request’s page. Every claim is a transaction you sign; nothing is ever sent without you.

### Your invite link

Next to your rank on your own profile is your [referral link](/earn/referrals). One click copies it.

## Linked wallets

Most traders use more than one wallet. Link them to your account and Corium treats them as one:

- **DUST** from all of them adds up on one line of the board, and is paid to your main wallet (the one you sign in with).
- Your **track record** combines their pump.fun history.
- Your profile shows your linked wallets. They’re public, like everything on a profile.

### Link a wallet

<ol class="steps">
  <li><b>Sign in</b> with your main wallet and open <b>Linked wallets</b> on your profile.</li>
  <li><b>Switch</b> your wallet app to the wallet you want to link, and connect it.</li>
  <li><b>Sign</b> the link message with that wallet. It names your account, the wallet and an expiry, and says signing moves nothing and costs nothing.</li>
</ol>

**Ledger** can’t sign plain messages on Solana, so for a Ledger you sign a transaction whose only instruction is a memo of that same message. It’s never sent to the chain, costs nothing and can’t move anything; Corium only checks the signature.

| | |
|---|---|
| Wallets per account | Up to 10 |
| One account per wallet | A wallet linked to someone’s account can’t be linked to another until it’s unlinked |
| Unlinking | Your account can unlink any of its wallets, and a linked wallet can leave on its own, at any time |

Linking proves ownership; it never gives Corium any access to the wallet.

## Track record: bring your pump.fun stats

Your pump.fun history is already public, on chain. Corium reads it from your wallets’ own transactions and shows it on your profile, so your record follows you here.

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **pump.fun trading** | Realized profit in SOL on pump.fun coins you traded (not ones you launched), your number of trades and coins, the share of coins you made money on, and how many graduations you helped finish |
| **pump.fun launches** | How many coins you launched, how many graduated, your graduation rate and what you made as their creator |

- **What’s read:** pump.fun’s own curve trades and launches, and PumpSwap trades after graduation, fees included, from up to the last 180 days.
- **How:** each wallet is imported once when it signs in or is linked, which takes a few seconds, then topped up with anything new.
- **Combined:** with linked wallets, the cards add up all of them.

It’s read-only: importing your history needs no signature beyond signing in, and changes nothing on chain.
