# The screen

<p class="lede">Every coin page on Corium, for Corium coins and pump.fun coins alike, carries a screen: the signals a trader checks before buying, read from the chain and public sources, so you don't have to leave to check.</p>

## What it reads

| Signal | What it is | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Bundlers** | Wallets that bought in the coin's **launch block**, the same block as the transaction that created it. Only a bundle lands there. Shown as what they bought and what they still hold. | The coin's first trades, read once and kept |
| **Snipers** | Wallets that bought within about **two seconds** of the launch block. | The same |
| **Top 10** | The ten largest holders' share of the supply, with pools and exchanges left out. | Jupiter |
| **Dev** | The creator's share now, what the creator bought at launch, and whether the creator has sold out. | Jupiter and the launch block |
| **Insiders** | Among the largest holders, wallets whose first SOL came from the **same wallet**, grouped, and what they hold together. A group funded by the creator is marked. Exchanges and bots, which fund thousands of unrelated wallets, never group anyone. | Corium's own trace of each holder's first transaction, with RugCheck's figure alongside as a cross-check |
| **Fresh wallets** | Top holders whose **first transaction** is from the day of the launch or later: wallets made for this coin. | The same trace |
| **Creator** | How many coins the creator launched, how many graduated, and whether the creator has rugged a token before. | Jupiter and RugCheck |
| **Authorities** | Whether the **mint** and **freeze** authorities are revoked. If not, more tokens can be printed, or wallets frozen. | Jupiter |
| **Organic** | Jupiter's estimate of how much of the volume is organic. | Jupiter |

Each signal is **green**, **amber** or **red** against fixed thresholds, and the verdict at the head of the screen is the worst of them.

| Signal | Amber from | Red from |
|---|---|---|
| Bundlers' holdings | 8% of supply | 20% |
| Snipers' holdings | 6% | 15% |
| Top 10 share | 30% | 50% |
| Dev's share | 4% | 10% |
| Insiders' holdings | 8% | 20% |
| Fresh wallets' holdings | 10% | 25% |
| Creator | 20+ coins launched and under 10% graduated | Has rugged before |
| Authorities | | Mint or freeze not revoked |
| Organic | Jupiter rates it low | |

A creator who bought at launch and has sold almost all of it is amber under **Dev**.

## Where you see it

- **The coin page.** The strip under the chart. Open **Details** for every signal's figure and the wallets the launch block names.
- **Holder and trade lists.** Wallets the screen knows are labelled: **Bundle**, **Sniper**, **Insider**, **Fresh**, **Dev**.
- **Before a buy.** On a coin rated red, Corium shows the red flags first. You decide; nothing is blocked.
- **The pump.fun list** on Bounties shows each coin's verdict as a dot, and can hide red-rated coins.
- **Cori** reads the same screen when you ask her about a coin.

## What Corium does with it

- The **free bounty** that a final-stretch buy unlocks is not offered on coins the screen rates red. Corium's own SOL stays off them.
- Corium's **X account** does not post coins the screen rates red.

## What it is not

The screen shows **signals**, not a verdict on anyone and not a guarantee. Tracing the holders' funding takes a few seconds the first time a coin is opened; a holder seen before costs nothing, and what wasn't reached in time is read on the next look. A label is a fact about a wallet's trades ("bought in the launch block"), never an accusation. A green screen does not mean a coin is safe: it means these particular signals are clear right now. Sources can be slow or down; a missing one is shown as unavailable, and the last good read stands meanwhile. Nothing here is financial advice.
