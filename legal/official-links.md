## Stay safe

::: danger We will never…
- message you first
- ask for your seed phrase or private key
- ask you to "validate", "sync" or "migrate" your wallet
- run an airdrop, bounty or refund that needs you to send SOL first
:::

- **Check the program ID** your wallet shows before you approve a transaction.
- **Signing in is a message, not a transaction.** If a "sign-in" asks you to approve a transfer, it isn't us.
- **Check the domain.** Official Corium sites are only on `corium.so` and its subdomains.
- **Check a coin's mint address, not its name.** Anyone can launch a coin called anything. [Safety →](/rules/safety)

## Reporting a vulnerability

Found a bug in a program or an app? Please email [corium.so@proton.me](mailto:corium.so@proton.me), or see [corium.so/.well-known/security.txt](https://www.corium.so/.well-known/security.txt). Please don't disclose it publicly until we've had a chance to fix it.
