# STARDUST

STARDUST is Corium's score. It's a counter on your player account, **not a token and not money**. You can't send it, sell it, or withdraw it. It powers the DUST leaderboard and unlocks perks in the app.

## How it's earned

```
STARDUST = 1,000 per SOL sent × stage multiplier × chance bonus
```

### Stage multiplier

The earlier in a star's life you send, the more dust each SOL earns.

| Stage | From mass | Multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| Protostar | 0 SOL | 2.5× |
| Main sequence | 2 SOL | 1.5× |
| Blue giant | 6 SOL | 1.3× |
| Red giant | 10 SOL | 1.15× |
| Supergiant | 14 SOL | 1.0× |
| Critical | 18 SOL | 0.9× |
| Event horizon | 21 SOL | 0.8× |

### Chance bonus

A push that buys a bigger slice of the star earns a little more dust. The bonus scales linearly between these points, based on that push's own nova chance:

| Nova chance | Bonus |
|---|---|
| 1% | 1.00× |
| 2% | 1.03× |
| 5% | 1.10× |
| 10% or more | 1.20× |

The bonus only affects dust. It **doesn't change your SOL odds** or your expected return.
