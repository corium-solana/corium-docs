# Safety

## Your funds

**Corium never holds your funds or your keys.** Every trade, launch and claim is a transaction you read and sign in your own wallet, and it executes on Solana. Transactions are final: Corium can't reverse, cancel or refund them.

Signing in is a **message**, not a transaction. It never moves anything.

## The risks of memecoins

- **Most memecoins go to zero.** Tokens launched on Corium are created by users and have no intrinsic value.
- **Anyone can launch.** A coin on Corium is not endorsed by Corium, however high it ranks.
- **Prices move with every trade.** Slippage, partial fills and failed transactions happen.
- **Creators can sell.** Check the **Dev** trades, the holder list and whether the creator bought a large bag at launch.
- **Smart contracts, RPC providers, wallets and Solana itself can fail.**

Only trade what you can afford to lose. You must be 18 or older and allowed to use a service like this where you live.

## What Corium guarantees, and what it doesn't

The bonding curve, graduation and the locked pool are Meteora's programs. The bounty escrow and every fee split are enforced by the Corium program: see [the fee router](/rules/fee-router) for exactly what it guarantees and what you trust.

Corium can hide coins and chat from its own interface. It cannot touch tokens or transactions on chain.

## Scams to watch for

::: danger Corium will never…
- message you first, in chat or anywhere else
- ask for your seed phrase or private key
- ask you to "validate", "sync" or "migrate" your wallet
- ask you to send SOL to receive a bounty, an airdrop or a refund
:::

- **Copycat coins.** Anyone can launch a coin with any name and image, including one that copies another coin. Check the **mint address**, not the name.
- **Links in chat** are shown as plain text on purpose. Think twice before copying one.
- **Impersonation.** Profile names can't look like wallet addresses or official accounts, but read the address on the profile if it matters.
- **Check the domain** before you connect a wallet. Official links are listed [here](/legal/official-links).

If you see a scam coin, impersonation or abuse in chat, tell us on [X](https://x.com/corium_so) or by [email](mailto:corium.so@proton.me).
